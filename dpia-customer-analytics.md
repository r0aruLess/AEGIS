## 1. Description of Processing & Architectural Context
CloudSphere provides enterprise B2B customers with a behavioral analytics SaaS platform. The system ingests high-frequency clickstream telemetry, user session paths, and in-app feature interactions generated by end-users of our enterprise clients.

The analytics engine processes this event stream to compute aggregate customer usage metrics, detect drop-off funnels, and calculate predictive user engagement and churn probability scores.

---

## 2. Necessity & Proportionality Assessment
*   **Purpose Limitation:** Telemetry processing is strictly limited to providing behavioral analytics and product improvement insights to the specific enterprise client whose SDK generated the event. Customer datasets are strictly segregated using multi-tenant VPC subnets and Aurora PostgreSQL tenant schemas. Zero co-mingling or cross-client data monetization occurs.
*   **Data Minimization:** CloudSphere SDKs strip raw IP addresses post-geo-resolution (IP masking to `/24` CIDR blocks). Direct personal identifiers (names, credit card numbers, national IDs) are blocked by automated regex filters at the ingestion API edge. Unique users are tracked via salted cryptographic hashes (`SHA-256(tenant_id + client_user_id + daily_salt)`).

## 3. Risk Assessment to Rights & Freedoms of Data Subjects

| Identified Privacy Threat | Inherent Likelihood | Inherent Impact | Inherent Risk | Technical & Organizational Safeguards (Mitigations) | Residual Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Re-Identification of Salted Identifiers:** Attacker correlates pseudonymous telemetry with external datasets to identify individuals. | 3 (Possible) | 3 (Moderate) | **Medium (9)** | Daily rotating salts for hash generation; strict tenant-level encryption keys; strict contract terms prohibiting clients from sending plain-text PII. | **Low (3)** |
| **Unauthorized Cross-Tenant Data Access:** Logical failure in multitenancy leads to Client A viewing Client B's analytics. | 2 (Unlikely) | 4 (Major) | **Medium (8)** | Row-Level Security (RLS) in Aurora PostgreSQL; automated unit tests for tenant isolation in CI/CD; automated IAM boundaries. | **Low (2)** |
| **Unlawful Automated Decision-Making:** Predictive churn model automatically restricts end-user service access without human review. | 2 (Unlikely) | 3 (Moderate) | **Medium (6)** | CloudSphere models produce advisory analytics dashboards only; CloudSphere does not execute automated adverse legal actions against end-users. | **Low (2)** |
