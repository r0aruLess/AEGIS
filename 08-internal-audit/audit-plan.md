# Annual Internal Audit Plan (FY 2026)
**Document Owner:** Internal Audit Lead / Independent Assessor  
**Target Organization:** CloudScale Technologies Inc.  
**Audit Standard:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (Clause 9.2) & ISO 19011:2018 Guidelines  
**Audit Period:** Q1 2026 (February 1 – February 28, 2026)

---

## 1. Audit Scope & Objectives
The objective of this internal audit is to evaluate the operational effectiveness of CloudScale's Information Security Management System (ISMS) across all 4 themes of ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A, ensuring preparedness for the upcoming Stage 2 external certification audit.

## 2. Auditor Independence
The internal audit is led by an independent certified auditor (ISO 27001 Lead Auditor) who does not directly own the design or daily operations of the controls under review.

## 3. Audit Schedule & Control Sampling
| Audit Week | Focus Area / Controls Sampled | Key Stakeholders Audited |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Organizational Controls (A.5.1, A.5.15, A.5.19, A.5.24) | CISO, Legal Counsel, Procurement |
| **Week 2** | People & Physical Controls (A.6.1-A.6.8, A.7.1-A.7.14) | Head of People/HR, Workplace Ops |
| **Week 3** | Technological Controls - Infrastructure & Cloud (A.8.1-A.8.14) | Lead DevOps Engineer, Cloud Architect |
| **Week 4** | Secure Development Lifecycle & Cryptography (A.8.20-A.8.34) | VP Engineering, Security Champions |
